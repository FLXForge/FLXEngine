Audio Chip

Index · Video · Audio · Input

Voices · Overflow · Synthesis · Fidelity · Resources and fileAudio

Audio Chip defines the capabilities and constraints of the audio system available to Sound and Music. It is declared in machine.audio. Specific sounds, songs and instruments belong to JSON Audio.

Structure
audio
├── voices: music, sound, mode, overflow
├── synthesis: model, texture, movement, noise
├── fidelity: resolution, dynamics, space
├── resources: generated, samples, streams
└── fileAudio: mode

Voices

Voice budget
FieldType / valuesDefaultBehavior in v0.3.0
voices.musicInteger ≥ 08Limit on music channels used. Channels exceeding the available voices are ignored with a warning.
voices.soundInteger ≥ 016Limit on simultaneous effects in reserved/preferred; in shared, contributes to the total pool size.
voices.modeshared · reserved · preferredsharedAllocation policy detailed below.
voices.overflowignore · replace_oldest · replace_newest · replace_lowest_priority · steal_from_musicreplace_oldestPolicy when requesting a sound after available voices are exhausted.
Voice allocation
ModeEffective behavior
reservedMusic and sounds have separate reserves: music limits music channels, and sound limits simultaneous effects.
sharedPool of music + sound voices. Effects and music compete for it; music also retains its music limit. Sounds can use the shared space, so sound is not an independent effects limit here.
preferredAccepted value. In v0.3.0 it uses the same functional policy as reserved; it has no distinct preference behavior.

With music: 0, no music channels are used. In shared mode, sound: 0 does not necessarily disable effects: shared capacity contributed by music may remain.

Overflow

When a new Sound arrives and there is no capacity
ValueResult
ignoreIgnores the new request.
replace_oldestReplaces the oldest active sound.
replace_newestReplaces the newest active sound.
replace_lowest_priorityReplaces the active sound with the lowest Sound.priority; at equal priority, the oldest. Selects a victim among active sounds: does not guarantee rejection of an incoming request with lower priority.
steal_from_musicIn shared mode, can suspend active music to admit the sound. If no music can be suspended, ignores the request. In reserved/preferred, a full effects budget does not take music voices.

The replace policies select active sounds, not music channels. If the shared pool is full and no sound can be replaced, the new request is ignored. The policy applies to Sound requests; it does not promise to evict effects to start Music.

The implementation of steal_from_music pauses the active music mix, not an isolated channel. Resumes it when no active effects remain, unless music remains paused for another reason. Does not guarantee a permanent reserve for effects or selection of individual music channels.

Shared voices
machine:
  audio:
    voices:
      music: 3
      sound: 1
      mode: shared
      overflow: steal_from_music

Synthesis

Declared synthesis and current effect
FieldType / valuesDefaultBehavior in v0.3.0
synthesis.modelbuzzer · pulse · wave · mixed · fm · sample · openopenpulse adapts certain waveforms: sine becomes triangle and saw becomes pulse. The other values do not select seven independent synthesizers in the current implementation.
synthesis.textureraw · coarse · clean · rough · richrichModifies synthesis character and texture. Some values share treatment; they do not represent separate engines.
synthesis.movementnone · simple · dynamic · expressiveexpressiveModulates the intensity of movements and modulations. none restricts them; dynamic and expressive do not receive distinct treatment in this current factor.
synthesis.noisenone · simple · richrichModulates noise behavior. none removes noise components and transforms noise sources into an oscillator source; this is not equivalent to silencing the entire resource.
Synthesis profile
machine:
  audio:
    synthesis:
      model: pulse
      texture: clean
      movement: simple
      noise: simple

The sample model accepted in this field does not imply file playback or an implemented sample synthesizer. The declarations of source and tone still describe the sound content on which the chip acts.

Fidelity

Fidelity
FieldType / valuesDefaultBehavior in v0.3.0
fidelity.resolutionvery_low · low · medium · highhighLimits and quantizes amplitude resolution. high preserves the highest resolution of the current generator.
fidelity.dynamicsfixed · limited · normal · expressiveexpressivefixed and limited apply specific dynamics treatment. normal and expressive do not select different algorithms at this stage.
fidelity.spacemono · stereostereoAccepted and stored field; current generated synthesis remains mono, even with stereo.
Reduced fidelity
machine:
  audio:
    fidelity:
      resolution: low
      dynamics: limited
      space: mono

Resources and fileAudio

Properties without full functional enforcement
FieldType / valuesDefaultBehavior in v0.3.0
resources.generatedBooleantrueDeclares generated audio capability; AudioSystem does not currently enforce this flag as a complete generation block.
resources.samplesBooleantrueAccepted and stored; does not guarantee effective enabling or restriction of samples.
resources.streamsBooleantrueAccepted and stored; does not guarantee effective enabling or restriction of streams.
fileAudio.modeoff · sound · music · allallAccepted and stored; does not, by itself, enable file playback or guarantee it is blocked.

These fields describe accepted format, not constraints guaranteed by AudioSystem v0.3.0. Changing them should not be presented as a functional way to enable or disable WAV, OGG or MP3. This is independent of voice budgets, which do have an effect.
